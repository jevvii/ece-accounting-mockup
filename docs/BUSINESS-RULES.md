# Business rules — mockup mapping

Rule IDs are local to this mockup. Each maps to the domain code that owns the rule, the
justification, and an acceptance case. Synthetic fixture policies are labelled; they are not
current Philippine law. See `docs/DECISIONS.md` for open production choices.

| ID | Rule | Owner / command | Rationale and acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|
| BR-01 | One active workspace is one legal company. Cross-company reads, writes, archives and detail routes are rejected or filtered. | `shared/access.ts`, every command via `ensureActorAccess`; `queries.ts` workspace filters | Prevents accidental tenant mixing. G15; `access.test.ts`, `ledger.test.ts`, `archive.test.ts`. |
| BR-02 | Money is integer PHP centavos; quantities are thousandths; time is minutes; rates are basis points. Round half-up only at a named boundary. | `shared/money.ts`, `shared/dates.ts`, module calculators | Floating-point ledger totals are not acceptable. `money.test.ts`; G07/G11 residual cases. |
| BR-03 | Every state change passes through `executeCommand`; a rejection returns the prior snapshot unchanged. | `commands.ts`, `ledger.ts` | One auditable seam, atomic failures. `commands.test.ts`, negative tests in every module. |
| BR-04 | A request key is idempotent; reusing it with a different payload is a typed conflict. | `executeCommand` receipt map + payload hash | Network/UI retries must not duplicate records. G02; `commands.test.ts`. |
| BR-05 | A business source effect is separately guarded (`invoice-post:…`, `payroll-post:…`, stock effects). A new request key cannot repeat an effect. | `ledger.effectRecordKey`, `markEffect`, module effect keys | Two-layer duplicate defence. G02, G11 repeat post, payroll repeat, CSV re-import. |
| BR-06 | Posted financial records are immutable. Corrections are linked reversals or credit/adjustment documents in an open period. | `ledger.reverseJournal`, `billing.reverseInvoice`, `other.reversePostedJournal`, payroll reversal | Preserves history and audit. G14; `ledger.test.ts`, `billing.test.ts`. |
| BR-07 | Posting into a closed period is rejected; reopen requires an authorized user and a reason; both are audited. | `other.closePeriod` / `reopenPeriod`, `ledger.assertPeriodOpen` | Period control without rewriting entries. G14; `ledger.test.ts`, `reports.test.ts`. |
| BR-08 | Journals must balance exactly, use active same-company accounts, and have one non-zero side per line. | `ledger.postJournal` | Accounting integrity and isolation. `ledger.test.ts`. |
| BR-09 | Maker ≠ checker on payment requests, manual journal approval, payroll approval and bank-detail changes. A combined role does not permit self-approval. | `purchases.approveRfp`, `other.approveJournalDraft`, `payroll.approvePayroll`, `cash.undoBankRow` | Segregation of duties. | BR-12 | Accounting activation requires the exact approved opening value and happens once. Historical standalone movements are retained, not replayed. | `admin.activateAccounting`, `admin.importMasters` | G16; `admin.test.ts`, inventory G16 test, admin e2e. |
| BR-13 | An invoice posts AR/revenue/output VAT once; receipts allocate to invoices and excess is explicit unapplied credit; allocations may not exceed invoice balance. | `billing.createInvoice/postInvoice/createReceipt` | G01/G12; `billing.test.ts`, `billing-receipts.test.ts`. |
| BR-14 | An RFP creates no expense. Bills recognise expense/AP once; settlement clears AP and credits cash/clearing; bank clearing is a separate reviewed effect. | `purchases.createBill/createRfp/createPayment`, `cash.matchBankRow` | G03/G19; `purchases.test.ts`, `cash.test.ts`. |
| BR-15 | Employee advances release cash against an advance asset; liquidation expenses clear the advance; unused amounts return; excess becomes payable, never a payroll deduction. | `purchases.createAdvance/liquidateAdvance` | G04/G05; `purchases.test.ts`. |
| BR-16 | Inventory uses one company-wide moving-average pool per item; transfers do not change company value; full issue consumes the exact residual centavo; location-negative issues and out-of-order valued movements are rejected. | `inventory.record*`, `issueValue`, `movementPool` | G06/G07; `inventory.test.ts`, `inventory-returns.test.ts`. |
| BR-17 | Customer/supplier returns are capped by the original movement and valued from its issue/receipt value. Stock counts post absolute adjustments with a reason. | `inventory.recordCustomerReturn/SupplierReturn/Count` | `inventory-returns.test.ts`. |
| BR-18 | Generic bank CSV re-import adds no rows; amount mismatches remain unresolved; each match/undo posts once and preserves history. | `cash.importBankRows`, `matchBankRow`, `undoBankRow` | G17; `cash.test.ts`. |
| BR-19 | Payroll uses an explicit hourly basis, policy period and time matrix. Each time bucket is valued separately; base-included buckets add only the premium. | BR-21 | Extended worksheets use only explicit inputs: 13th month divisor, annualization prior-employer pay, final-pay components and draft 2316. No inferred entitlement or automatic advance deduction. | `payroll.computeWorksheet`, `saveWorksheet` | G10/G10a/G10b; `payroll.test.ts`, payroll e2e. |

| BR-24 | VAT/EWT/FWT registers distinguish output, creditable and final withholding. A Form 2307 register references creditable records only; final withholding never becomes a 2307. | `other.createFinalWithholding`, `createCertificate2307`, tax queries | G19; `tax.test.ts`. |
| BR-25 | A company archive contains only that company, uses a canonical checksum, previews before restore and rejects tampering, foreign records or mixed payloads atomically. | `archive.buildCompanyArchive/mergeCompanyArchive`, `archive.restore` | G18; `archive.test.ts`, admin e2e. |
| BR-26 | Maintenance mode blocks ordinary writes and restore while allowing authorized export/validation and ending the window. | `admin.setMaintenance`, `MAINTENANCE_ALLOWED_COMMANDS` | G18; `commands.test.ts`, admin e2e. |
| BR-27 | Corrupt, unsupported or invalid stored state is surfaced with raw export and confirmed reset; no silent reseed. Failed writes keep visible state. | `adapters/storage.ts`, persistence tests | G18; `persistence.test.ts`, navigation e2e. |
| BR-28 | Browser roles and grants are a simulation of authorization, not authentication or tenant security. | UI/access helpers, shell label | Prevents overclaiming. Documented in README/DECISIONS; no fake MFA/encryption indicators. |
| BR-29 | Public business codes are allocated by one numbering service from a per-company, per-record-kind policy: prefix plus a safe integer sequence, explicit minimum digits and positive increment. | BR-30 | Numbering policies are authoritative for current and new master codes. IDs, posted financial snapshots, issued document numbers, external references and source/idempotency keys remain preserved. | `masterCodes.reconcileMasterCodes`, `numbering.allocateMasterCode/updateNumberingPolicy`, `migration`, `admin.updateNumbering` | Owner supersedes prospective-only master coding. `master-policy-authority.test.ts`, numbering/restore tests and e2e. |
| BR-31 | Schema v1 state and archives migrate deterministically to v2. Migration preserves IDs, codes, balances, snapshots, source effects and receipts; it only backfills new public fields and builds policy counters above reserved codes. Invalid or future snapshots fail without replacement. | `domain/migration.ts`, `adapters/storage.ts`, `domain/archive.ts` | Safe continuation from the historical build. `numbering.test.ts`, `persistence.test.ts`, `archive.test.ts`. |
| BR-32 | Every posted journal belongs as a whole balanced entry to one of five source books (`sales`, `collections`, `purchases`, `cash_disbursements`, `journals`) via versioned `sourceProvenance`. Generic reversals (`journal_reversal`, `bank_clearing_undo`) inherit the root original journal's book even after bank match links are cleared; inventory valuation journals stay in `journals`; unrecognized or broken legacy provenance is classified into `journals` with `provisional: true` and surfaced as a review exception, never omitted. | `domain/schema.ts`, `domain/ledger.ts`, `domain/cash.ts`, `domain/migration.ts`, `domain/queries.ts` | Whole-journal double-entry integrity per book; no line-splitting or silent drops. `ece-trial-balance-oracle.test.ts` (11/11), `reports.test.ts`. |

## Synthetic fixture policies

These are deliberately named demo inputs, not statutory rules:

- 12% output/input VAT wiring profile.
- Time matrix factors from 100% ordinary regular through holiday/night/OT combinations.
- Simplified annual tax schedule: zero through PHP 100,000, then 10% of excess (basis points).
- 13th-month divisor 12 with an explicit eligibility input.
- Company-specific deduction schedules and payroll policy periods.
- Company B cutover opening value of PHP 1,000.00 and payroll expiry 2026-12-31.

The interface labels these as demo fixtures. Live use requires owner/accountant verification
of current Philippine law, provider rules and company policy.

Monthly activity restricts source-book columns to that month; displayed/exported closing balances include prior history. Custom dates remain available. | `shared/reportPeriod.ts`, `sourceBookTrialBalance`, Reports | Month/year/leap-day boundaries, drill/export parity; `report-period.test.ts`, `review-corrections.spec.ts`. |

A payroll-officer role alone is insufficient. | `journalVisibility.ts`, Reports/Other queries/views | `report-review-regressions.test.ts`; visible aggregate amounts remain unchanged. |
